Miiras ("we", "us") makes the Miiras conversation cards app. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version

The Miiras app does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no recordings and no analytics.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for your name, email or any other personal details.
  • It does not record audio, save answers or store anything you say.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • It does not send any data to us or to third parties.

Information on your device

While the app is open, it keeps track of which questions you've gone through so it can show your progress. This stays on your device, is never sent anywhere, and is cleared when the app is closed.

Contacting us

If you email us, we'll use your email address only to reply. Contact: hello@getmiiras.com.

Children

Miiras is designed for adults and their families. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If we change how the app handles information, for example by adding features that save data, we will update this policy and the date above before those changes go live.
