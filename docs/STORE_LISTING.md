# App Store listing draft

**App name (30 characters max):** Miiras: Family Conversations

**Subtitle (30 characters max):** Talk about what matters

**Category:** Lifestyle (secondary: Health & Fitness, or Finance)

**Promotional text (170 characters max):**
The money, health and end-of-life conversations families keep putting off, made easier. 100 questions to go through together, one card at a time.

**Description:**
Most families agree these conversations matter. Few have them.

Miiras gives your family a gentle, structured way to talk about the things that are hard to bring up: money, health, care, wills, fairness and legacy. Sit down together, open a deck and go through one question at a time.

• 10 decks and 100 questions, grounded in research on family communication, from easy openers to deeper wishes
• Start with "Opening the conversation" to agree how you'll talk
• Swipe when you've talked a question through, or skip it for later
• No amounts needed, ever
• Nothing is recorded, saved or shared. What's said at the table stays there

Decks: Opening the conversation, What matters most, Everyday money, Will you be okay?, Health and medical wishes, Care and living later, Wills and important documents, Inheritance and fairness, Who does what, End of life and legacy.

Miiras helps families talk. It isn't medical, legal or financial advice.

**Review notes for Apple:**
Miiras is a conversation-card app for families. Open any deck, tap "Start the conversation", then swipe right on a card when it's been discussed or left to skip. No login is required and no data is collected. The questions draw on published research and public guides on family communication and end-of-life planning.
